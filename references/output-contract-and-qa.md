# Output contract and quality gates

## Exact output sequence

Use these eight sections in exactly this order.

1. `# 1. 内容诊断` — topic value, core conflict, target reader, communication angle; name the evidence basis or verification status when factual claims are used.
2. `# 2. 核心观点提炼` — one sentence beginning `这个主题真正重要的是……`; never use “我的判断是”.
3. `# 3. 卡片规划` — state the recommended number of cards and, for every card, give `主题`, `核心信息`, and `视觉方向`.
4. `# 4. 图片 Prompt` — a separate, complete prompt for every card containing Design Language, Color System, Layout, Visual Metaphor, Text/Information Structure, and Brand Requirement.
5. `# 5. 小红书标题` — five titles: one `推荐标题` and four `备选`; each ≤20 Chinese characters.
6. `# 6. 小红书正文` — 850–950 Chinese characters, <1,000; fact + explanation + viewpoint + reader value, using the five-part body flow.
7. `# 7. 标签` — 5–8 targeted tags.
8. `# 8. 发布前质量检查` — report every checklist result below. Do not mark a failed or unverified item as passed.

Do not add a final conclusion section after section 8. Put sources or caveats inline in the relevant earlier section, rather than changing the required response order.

## Evidence gate

Before release, verify:

- Every factual claim has a real source, supplied material provenance, or explicit uncertainty label.
- Facts are not confused with the author viewpoint.
- Trend claims are not exaggerated or causally overstated.

Where browsing or source material is unavailable, remove unsupported exact claims; retain only carefully framed general explanation and clearly mark any inference. Never invent a source, citation, date, statistic, quote, research ID, issue number, or URL.

## Final QA checklist

Report the following items with truthful `✅`, `⚠️`, or `❌` status and a concise reason for any non-pass.

### 内容质量

- 事实准确
- 有明确观点
- 有认知价值

### 写作质量

- 无AI套话
- 无咨询报告腔
- 作者声音自然

### 视觉质量

- 风格统一
- 配色独立
- 3:4比例
- 品牌完整
- 知识档案版式完整（米白纸感底、左上大标题、侧边图例、中央系统图、下部证据模块、结论/品牌锁定）
- 成品尺寸为 1080 × 1440（精确 3:4）
- 右下角仅为 `「两克伴」出品`（无空格、无替代引号、无额外符号）

Also check that every page has one cognitive task, the reading path is clear, text is not too dense, the subject is abstract/symbolic, no prohibited visual element appears, and the bottom-right mark is present.
