# 两克伴知识档案版式模板

## Reference assets

Use `assets/style-references/knowledge-archive-01.png` through `knowledge-archive-04.png` as canonical references. Preserve their hierarchy, asymmetry, white space, and single dominant technical visual; do not reproduce their topic or wording.

## First rule: one page, one dominant argument

Create an editorial knowledge page, not a dashboard, slide, generic infographic, or three-column explainer. Every page has **one visual centre of gravity**: one central technical diagram, comparison chart, layered system, or process model. It must occupy at least 30% of the usable page area and carry the page’s main claim.

Do not divide the canvas into three equal explanatory columns. Do not give every section the same visual weight. A narrow side rail is metadata, not a content column; evidence modules support the hero diagram, not compete with it.

## Fixed spatial grammar

Use a 1080 × 1440, 3:4 page with a 56–64 px outer margin and a 12-column underlying grid. Maintain these approximate regions; use them as proportions, not rigid boxes.

| Region | Position / share | Required role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Editorial header | upper-left, 5–44% width, 5–25% height | optional real card serial beside the title, 2–4 line heavy Chinese headline, short subhead, one palette rule; this is the largest typography on the page |
| Hero diagram | centre/right, 40–82% width, 8–46% height | one large explanatory system, chart, layered model, or topology; use scale, void, and fine structure |
| Legend rail | right outer 82–95% width, 5–38% height | 4–6 concise topic-specific marks; icon + label only, with generous spacing |
| Evidence band | 5–95% width, 48–82% height | one subordinate pattern chosen below; never a full equal-width dashboard |
| Conclusion / footer | 5–95% width, 84–96% height | one strong conclusion block or process strip, continuing relation texture and rules, exact bottom-right brand lock-up |

## Header and hero relationship

The headline must dominate the upper-left. The hero must dominate the centre/right. These two masses create the primary tension of the page. Keep 15–25% of the upper canvas as quiet paper/field around them; do not fill every gap with cards, text, or diagrams. Do not create a left vertical magazine rail containing version, date, author, review, or other decorative metadata. Use that area as margin and breathing room unless the card has genuine topic evidence to place there.

Use heavy grotesk Chinese sans-serif for the headline. Use thin rules, small labels, dot grids, and compact icons only as secondary material. Avoid coloured title bars, equal-sized cards, rounded UI panels, and generic dashboard headers.

## Choose one evidence pattern

Choose exactly one pattern according to the card’s cognitive task. Do not combine all patterns.

1. **Architecture cover** — use a lower-left quote/conclusion block, 3–5 small explanatory modules aligned to the hero, and one bottom process strip. Use for system introductions and first cards.
2. **Decision matrix** — use one full-width, ruled comparison matrix in the evidence band. Use for choices, myths, boundaries, or “when to use” pages.
3. **Failure / process map** — use one broad horizontal flow with one exception or recovery branch. Use for mechanisms, lifecycle, operations, and governance.
4. **Framework essay** — use two asymmetric evidence panels and a short bottom formula/sequence. Use for conceptual distinctions and viewpoint pages.

Keep all evidence subservient to the hero: no more than 35% of the page may be occupied by tabular or card-based evidence.

## Graphic vocabulary

Use thin construction lines, black/Ink nodes, small signal nodes, dotted matrices, square pixel fragments, translucent architecture planes, simple line icons, arrows, and sparse calibrated labels. Do not use 3D product rendering, app UI cards, glossy glass, large rounded containers, photos, decorative illustrations, neon, or a full-page coloured backdrop.

## Palette application

Apply the selected Color System as a complete visual field: canvas changes the paper field; Ink changes headline, rails, table rules and conclusion mass; Structure changes secondary planes and factual modules; Signal marks decisive differences. Preserve the same spatial grammar across palettes, but never collapse the palette into a few recoloured nodes.

## Prompt lock

Every prompt must state: `两克伴知识档案版式模板`, `one dominant central technical visual`, `large upper-left Chinese headline`, `narrow left metadata rail`, `narrow right legend rail`, the selected evidence pattern, `asymmetric editorial composition`, `large quiet paper/field areas`, and the palette’s `canvas / ink / structure / signal` mapping.

For the topic “知识库、Wiki、Memory 到底有什么区别？”, select **Framework essay**: use one central relationship diagram with “document source → compiled Wiki” as the main relationship and a separate personal-memory branch; use two asymmetric lower evidence panels and one concise bottom distinction formula. Do not lay out three equal columns or a decorative left metadata rail.

## Finalization and QA

Reserve only a small lower-right brand-safe area (about 18% of page width × 4% of page height). Do not draw a box, dashed rectangle, placeholder label, or empty panel around it. In the first image-generation prompt, explicitly require the exact brand text `「两克伴」出品` there, at micro-footer scale, aligned to the footer baseline and visually integrated with the fine rule, dots/pixels, or process line. It must be rendered natively by the image generation, not overlaid after generation. Continue the footer with a thin horizontal rule, sparse dot/pixel texture, a tiny relationship diagram, or the final process line so the bottom remains intentionally structured.

Release only exactly 1080 × 1440 px. Reject and regenerate if the page becomes a three-column dashboard, lacks a dominant hero visual, makes the legend rail a content column, fills the page uniformly, uses equal-sized modules, loses its upper-left headline hierarchy, uses a non-3:4 ratio, or displays any brand text other than `「两克伴」出品`.
