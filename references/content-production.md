# Content production rules

## Diagnose before writing

Answer all four questions internally, then surface the result in the content diagnosis:

1. Why is this topic worth attention?
2. What is the real question readers need to understand?
3. What differs from common understanding?
4. What judgment will readers gain after reading?

Do not start from empty concept explanation, “AI 正在改变世界”, or a source-only summary. Extract the core definition, emergence context, key change, mechanism, difference from past practice, use cases, common misconceptions, and future judgment.

## Card-count rubric

| Complexity | Cards | Use for | Required arc |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Level 1: simple concept | 5–6 | Open Weights, MCP, a single AI term | cover → definition → mechanism → value → summary |
| Level 2: complex technical topic | 7–9 | Graph Engineering, Context Engineering, Agent architecture | cover → background → concept → architecture → comparison → application → viewpoint summary |
| Level 3: system-level topic | 10–12 | complete AI Agent system, enterprise AI transformation, paradigm shift | multi-layer system explanation | 

Use the smallest count that preserves the required cognitive steps. A card cannot carry more than one primary cognitive task. Cover cards establish the question; subsequent cards must advance the answer rather than repeat it.

## Author voice and depth

Maintain 50% professional depth and 50% popular comprehension. Explain jargon in plain language without diluting its meaning. Separate:

- **事实**: sourced, falsifiable claims.
- **解释**: why the fact or mechanism matters.
- **两克伴观点**: a clearly signaled but natural authorial interpretation.

Prefer `我觉得…`, `我认为…`, `我感觉…`, `我看完之后最大的感受是…`, `我更倾向于…`, `在我看来…`, and `我的理解是…`. Never use `我的判断是`, `笔者认为`, `本文认为`, `根据我的判断`, or `综上所述`.

Avoid consultant language, academic detachment, generic AI phrasing, empty slogans, and strings of quotable sentences that have no explanation.

## Xiaohongshu deliverables

### Titles

Provide exactly five titles, no more than 20 Chinese characters each. Mark exactly one as `推荐标题`; show the other four as alternatives. Create a real reason to click without clickbait or revealing the whole answer. Prefer cognitive-conflict, counterintuitive, or question forms, such as “Graph 比 Loop 更高级吗？”, “AI 越强，人为什么越焦虑？”, and “为什么 AI Agent 开始需要 Graph？”.

### Body

Write approximately 850–950 Chinese characters and never more than 1,000. Use this five-part flow:

1. **热点/问题切入**: begin immediately with an observed change, conflict, or question. Never use “随着时代发展…” or “在人工智能快速发展的今天…”.
2. **核心概念解释**: define it, why it appeared, and the problem it solves.
3. **关键观点拆解**: explain mechanism, contrast with the previous way, application, and misconceptions.
4. **两克伴观点**: add a natural first-person author judgment.
5. **总结**: leave a judgment, action suggestion, or worthwhile question.

Make the copy fact-supported, opinionated, understandable, collectible, and shareable. Do not hide the source of a factual claim.

### Tags

Provide 5–8 effective tags composed of topic keyword + industry keyword + reader-interest keyword, including `#两克伴` when appropriate. Avoid broad filler such as `#科技`, `#互联网`, and `#未来`.
