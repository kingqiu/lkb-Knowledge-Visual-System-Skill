# Acceptance tests

Run all three cases after a material change. Generate a full package, then evaluate it against `output-contract-and-qa.md`. Do not treat a mechanically valid package as factually or visually approved without reviewing it.

## Case 1 — technical architecture

**Prompt:** `MCP（Model Context Protocol）是什么？为什么它可能成为 AI 时代的 USB-C？`

**Verify:** technical explanation, abstract-concept visualization, and information architecture. Expect a Level 1 or Level 2 decision justified by scope, a connector/interface metaphor rather than a robot, and a qualified comparison rather than an unsupported declaration of standard status.

## Case 2 — business trend

**Prompt:** `AI 原生公司的组织结构会是什么样？`

**Verify:** business analysis, abstract organization-structure visualization, and non-technical adaptation. Expect uncertainty to be explicit: this is a developing pattern, not a settled organizational fact. The system may introduce a commercial palette only under the palette constraints.

## Case 3 — cognition and humanities

**Prompt:** `为什么 AI 时代，人越来越焦虑？`

**Verify:** emotional-topic handling, humanistic expression, and branded point of view. Expect Warm Earth Shell by default, an abstract pressure/choice/feedback-system metaphor rather than a portrait, and no clinical or causal overclaim.

## Passing threshold

Each package passes only when it contains all eight required sections, chooses its card count rather than assuming it, has five compliant titles, keeps the body under 1,000 Chinese characters, has 5–8 tags, includes every prompt component, contains no prohibited author phrases or visual red flags, and receives a truthful final QA result. Review sources and visual logic manually.
